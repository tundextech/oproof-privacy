OProof Privacy Policy

TDIGITA HUB · Last updated: October 2026

What stays on your phone

Your customers' names and phone numbers, and every debt and payment you record. These are never uploaded to us. If you add a customer from your phone contacts, OProof opens your phone's own contact picker. OProof cannot see your contact list: it receives only the name and number of the one contact you tap, and your contact list is never uploaded. They leave your phone only when you share a backup, statement, receipt or reminder yourself.

What we receive

When you finish setup, we receive your country and business name. If you fill in My Profile, we also receive your full name, phone number, gender, date of birth and email. Along with these we receive a random install ID, the app version, your device type (such as Android, iPhone or web) and the time you accepted this policy. Fields you leave empty are not sent.

Your agreement

By tapping Get started you accept this Privacy Policy, including the sharing described above. Sharing continues while it is switched on in Settings.

Why we collect it

To understand who uses OProof and where, so we can improve the app and serve our users better, and to contact you about your feedback or support requests. We do not sell your information.

Where it is stored

In a cloud database provided by Google Firebase. Google acts as our service provider.

How long we keep it

While you use OProof with sharing on. When you turn sharing off in Settings, or ask us to delete it, we remove your record from our database.

Your choices

You can switch sharing off at any time in Settings. This stops further sharing and deletes your record from our database. You can edit or clear your profile details, and you can ask us to correct or delete your data by email. OProof works the same whether sharing is on or off.

Messages you send us

When you use Feedback and suggestions or Contact us, your message and any reply details you type are sent to our email address so we can respond.

Security

You can protect the app with a PIN, fingerprint and a secret question. These lock the screen but do not encrypt the data stored on your phone, so keep your phone safe and back up regularly.

Notifications

Due-date alerts are created on your phone from your own records. Nothing about them is sent to us.

Changes

If we change what we collect or why, we will update this policy and tell you in the app.

How to delete your OProof data

OProof (by TDigita Hub) keeps your customers, debts and payments only on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them. We never receive them.

To delete the profile data we hold on our server:

Option 1: Open OProof, go to Settings and switch off data sharing. Your record is deleted from our database straight away.

Option 2: Email tundextech@gmail.com from the address on your profile with the subject "Delete my OProof data". Include your business name. We delete your record within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted: country, business name, full name, phone number, gender, date of birth, email, install ID, app version and device type.

What we may keep: any feedback or contact messages you emailed us to support you.
